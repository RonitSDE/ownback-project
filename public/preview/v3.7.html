THE ONCHAIN RULES · NO FALSE FINALITY

Four stages. One honest outcome.

The mock-stock pilot has explicit payment and delivery evidence. Claims are reserved until eligible; a late refund after delivery must be accounted for instead of magically reversing a token transfer.

01 / RESERVEPayment verified

USDC receipt or authenticated Shopify paid-order webhook; reserve merchant reward funds.

02 / ELIGIBLEHold completed

Confirm payment is still valid, no cancellation, no refund, and required recipient authorization.

03 / SETTLINGTransaction under way

Wait for onchain confirmation. A submitted job or email is not token delivery.

04 / DELIVEREDOwnership recorded

Check actual token transfer receipt. Testnet synthetic rewards are not real securities.

What OWNBACK does not claim today

No genuine B20 equity issuance or claim rights, no verified live merchant pilot, no production Shopify checkout or automated Bankr borrowing in this static preview. The source retains read-only Bankr research and UCP catalog/cart integration for approved connected merchants.
